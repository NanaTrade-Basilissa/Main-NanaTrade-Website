About Basilissa

More than a
restaurant.

Since 2009, Basilissa has grown from its first restaurant into a network of six branches across Greater Accra — a subsidiary of Nanatrade Limited, built on disciplined excellence, ethical leadership and dependable service.

Our focus is simple: quality food, served consistently, with genuine care for every customer. Our ambition is larger — to become Africa’s foremost premium food brand and, in time, a global food franchise.
